## Abstract

This research investigates the Artificial Bee Colony (ABC) algorithm for optimizing drone delivery networks in urban environments. It compares ABC with traditional path-finding approaches such as Dijkstra's and A* algorithms.

Through experimental implementation across four strategic delivery hubs in Chandigarh for 110 origin-destination pairs, the findings show:

- ABC achieved superior route optimization in 53.6% of test cases (shorter route length)
- Average route distance:
  - ABC: 3029.22 meters  
  - Other approaches: 3052.22 meters  
- Approximate improvement: 25–30 meters per route

However, this improvement comes with a trade-off:

- ABC execution time: 140.41 seconds  
- Dijkstra's execution time: 36.43 seconds  
- ABC is approximately 2.8 times slower

Additionally, with an optimized drone population, delivery times were reduced by 33.14%.

---

## Key Insight

The study highlights a trade-off between optimization quality and computational efficiency:

- ABC is effective for complex, multi-constraint delivery scenarios due to its balance between exploration and exploitation
- Higher computational cost makes it less suitable for resource-constrained systems with limited onboard processing
